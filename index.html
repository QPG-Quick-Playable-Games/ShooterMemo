Instructions

Controls: Move with WASD or Arrow keys. Aim with mouse. Left click to shoot. In multiplayer open another tab to join.

Shop

Open Shop to buy or equip guns. Coins earned by killing enemies in singleplayer and multiplayer (personal).

Demo multiplayer

Host election: first tab becomes the host. Host spawns enemies and broadcasts enemy states. Other tabs render the same enemies.

Leader board (singleplayer)

At game over submit your name and wave to the leaderboard. Leaderboard is synced across tabs using BroadcastChannel and persists in localStorage. Export/Import to share manually across devices.
Demo: client-only. Leaderboard sync works across tabs in same browser via BroadcastChannel. No external services.
